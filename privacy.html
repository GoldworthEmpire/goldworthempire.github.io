Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Goldworth Empire website and Goldworth Publisher, the private tool the Goldworth Empire team uses to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel.

Who uses Goldworth Publisher

Only the owner of the Goldworth Empire channel. The tool is not available to the public and does not collect data from viewers, subscribers or any third party.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives authorisation to upload videos to that channel and to manage those uploads (title, description, visibility, schedule and upload status). It uses YouTube API Services for this purpose only.

What is stored

The authorisation tokens issued by Google and a log of our own uploads (video ID, title, time and status) are stored on a computer controlled by the channel owner. They are not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone, and are not used for advertising or analytics about individuals.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the tool is retired.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no tracking or analytics scripts. The company hosting it may keep standard server logs.

Third-party terms

Goldworth Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
