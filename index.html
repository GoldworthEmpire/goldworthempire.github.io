Making Money. Making Trouble.

Goldworth Empire is an original animated business comedy. Sir Sterling Goldworth is an eccentric British capitalist who believes everything can be monetised and calls every disaster a “pivot”. It is comedy, not financial advice.

Watch on YouTube and Instagram.

Goldworth Publisher

Goldworth Publisher is the private publishing tool used by the Goldworth Empire team. Its only function is to upload our own finished episodes to the Goldworth Empire YouTube channel, set their title, description and scheduled time, and read back the status of those uploads. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
